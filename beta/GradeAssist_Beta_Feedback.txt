This is an early teacher-feedback build. Please focus on how useful, clear,
and easy the program feels rather than visual perfection.

1. What did you like most?

2. What was confusing or difficult to use?

3. Which feature would be most useful to you or your students?

4. Is there anything you expected the program to do that it does not do yet?

5. Were any labels, instructions, or results unclear?

6. Did anything appear broken or behave unexpectedly?

7. Would you realistically use or recommend a tool like this?

8. Anything else you would change?
